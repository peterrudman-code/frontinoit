Execution Strategy

Speed Without Compromising Governance

Quick deployment does not mean taking operational shortcuts.

Many midmarket implementations fail because companies try to rebuild their manual, Excel-based legacy habits inside a modern ERP. Frontino IT enforces a disciplined, standard-first approach. We guide your team to adopt standardized SAP configurations, minimizing customization risks, and accelerating delivery timelines without losing process control.

The GROW Fast Principles:

  • Predefined Scope: Strictly defined packages eliminate unexpected change requests.
  • Fast Decision Cycles: Structured standard alignments reduce design workshops.
  • Risk Governance: Immediate sandbox testing highlights process issues early.

Our Path

The GROW Fast Methodology

A structured, milestone-driven execution cycle designed to deploy your system in weeks, not months.

01

Discover Express

Determine system readiness, validate licensing needs, and define package parameters quickly.

02

Prepare Sprint

Stand up development sandbox environments and configure initial user access roles.

03

Fit-to-Standard

Review pre-configured best-practice processes directly in the sandbox system.

04

Realize Sprints

Configure ledgers, map interfaces, cleanse master data, and execute validation scripts.

05

Deploy

Train business users, execute cutover checklists, load master balances, and go-live.

06

Run

Ensure business stability with direct support desk coverage and transition to operations.
