What We Solve

Our SAP BTP Services

Each service starts from a concrete business problem. The technology comes last — it is how we deliver the outcome, not the outcome itself.

Integration

Problem
Processes still depend on email, manual file transfers or fragile point-to-point interfaces that break without warning.
What we do
We connect SAP with external systems through governed, monitored integration scenarios — with alerting and error runbooks from day one.
Outcome
Less manual work, fewer interface failures and full traceability of what moved where.
Technology
SAP Integration Suite, APIs, events and Cloud Connector.

Automation & workflows

Problem
Approvals and document handling run through inboxes and spreadsheets, with no audit trail and long cycle times.
What we do
We model the process, automate decisions with business rules and route exceptions to the right people inside a governed workflow.
Outcome
Faster cycles, compliant audit logs and teams focused on exceptions instead of routine.
Technology
SAP Build Process Automation, business rules and document information extraction.

Extensions, portals & apps

Problem
The business needs custom screens, portals or logic — but modifying the ERP core blocks upgrades and raises risk.
What we do
We build side-by-side applications, supplier or customer portals and dashboards that talk to SAP through standard APIs.
Outcome
New capabilities without touching the core, and an ERP that stays upgrade-ready.
Technology
SAP Build, CAP, SAP Fiori / UI5 and SAP BTP runtime services.

Data & enterprise AI

Problem
Knowledge is scattered across documents and tickets, and data needed for decisions is hard to reach or trust.
What we do
We bring SAP data together and implement governed AI assistants for support, documentation search and process lookups.
Outcome
Quicker answers, better decisions and AI use that stays within your security and compliance boundaries.
Technology
SAP AI Core, generative AI hub, SAP HANA Cloud and SAP Datasphere.

Clean core & operations

Problem
Years of custom code make every upgrade expensive, and nobody owns the health of extensions and interfaces after go-live.
What we do
We assess custom code, move logic side-by-side where it makes sense, and monitor and run the BTP landscape under clear SLAs.
Outcome
Predictable upgrades, stable operations and a landscape that keeps improving instead of degrading.
Technology
SAP BTP cockpit, Cloud ALM, ATC custom-code checks and monitoring tools.
The Transformation

Moving to a Clean SAP Architecture

Compare the operational drag of traditional customized setups with the stability of a BTP-extended environment.

Traditional Legacy Drag:

  • Manual approvals scattered across offline email boxes.
  • Fragile, custom-coded system interfaces that break frequently.
  • Shadow IT applications built without corporate security compliance.
  • Slow IT delivery times due to complex core code interdependencies.
  • Custom ABAP code cluttering the ERP core, blocking upgrades.

Governed BTP Environment:

  • Governed Workflows: Real-time approval tracking and automated audit trails.
  • Upgrade-Safe Integrations: Decoupled interfaces mapping via Integration Suite standard APIs.
  • Unified Governance: Secure access boundaries controlled centrally in SAP BTP.
  • Rapid Application Delivery: Quick prototype cycles built on clean low-code frameworks.
  • Clean ERP Core: Worry-free standard upgrades with zero code maintenance drag.
