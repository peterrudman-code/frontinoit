The Problem

Most GROW with SAP projects start with the wrong assumptions.

When discovery is skipped or rushed, scope creep, rework, and missed go-live dates follow. These are the signals that a structured workshop should come first.

Unknown process fit

You don't know which of your current processes SAP standard covers and which require a workaround, extension, or scope exclusion — so estimates are guesses.

Inflated or vague vendor proposals

Implementation proposals that differ by 3× in cost and timeline signal that no one has actually validated the scope — they are selling a project, not a fit.

Business and IT not aligned

Finance, operations, and IT each have a different picture of what the project should deliver. Without a shared baseline, decisions stall and requirements drift.

No clean-core baseline

Extension risk is not assessed before the project starts. Custom objects are carried into the new system by default, increasing costs and blocking future upgrades.

Unrealistic timeline expectations

Stakeholders commit to a go-live date before scope is defined. When reality hits during the project, delays and budget overruns follow — and the team takes the blame.

How It Works

Three focused days. Three structured outputs.

Each day has a defined agenda, participant list, and documented output. We run the workshop on-site, remote, or hybrid — adapted to your team's setup.

Day 1

Business Process Discovery

  • Current-state walkthrough of Finance, Procurement, Sales, and Operations
  • Key pain points, manual workarounds, and process exceptions documented
  • Decision authority mapping — who owns which process
  • Existing customizations and integrations catalogued
Output: Current-state process map and pain point register
Day 2

Fit-to-Standard Analysis

  • Process-by-process comparison against SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition standard
  • Gap identification: configuration options vs. BTP extensions vs. exclusions
  • Clean-core posture assessment per process area
  • Integration points and third-party dependencies reviewed
Output: Fit matrix draft and gap resolution options log
Day 3

Roadmap & Decision Session

  • Scope boundary definition: in, deferred, excluded
  • Phased milestone plan with indicative budget ranges
  • Resource model and internal readiness review
  • Executive readout: go / no-go recommendation with evidence
Output: Implementation roadmap and final workshop report
