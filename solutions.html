Products we build around SAP

Beyond consulting projects, we engineer our own enterprise solutions. Vendor Date Hub is the one we currently show in most depth.

Frontino product

Vendor Date Hub (VDH)

Supplier collaboration and purchase-order confirmation orchestration around SAP. Whether suppliers answer through a portal or by email, PDF or Excel in any language, VDH interprets the response, matches it at PO line level, validates it against SAP and business rules, and writes it back under control. Buyers only handle the exceptions. Built on SAP BTP, without modifying the SAP core.

Automation

Approval Workflow Automation

The Challenge

Critical business approvals (like purchase orders and capital expenditures) ran via email chains. This caused process latency, a lack of audit trails, and data discrepancies.

Our Solution

Configured SAP Build Process Automation to trigger multi-level approval workflows, combining business rules and custom UI forms without changing the ERP core.

Business Impact

85% Faster Approvals

Reduced cycles from days to minutes. Provided 100% compliant, automated audit logs directly within SAP.

Integration

BTP Integration Sprint

The Challenge

Fragile, custom-coded system connectors and manual file replication between SAP and legacy platforms resulted in frequent interface failures.

Our Solution

Configured SAP Integration Suite to build standardized, decoupled data maps with proactive error-alerting runbooks and API logs.

Business Impact

99.9% Reliability

Eliminated manual data transfers and established real-time, proactive error visibility across connected systems.

Clean Core

Clean-Core Extension Review

The Challenge

Our Solution

Business Impact

60% Custom Code Retired

Achieved an upgrade-safe system core, enabling standard cloud updates with minimal testing effort.
