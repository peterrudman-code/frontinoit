SAP Cloud ERP

Know if your company is ready for SAP Cloud — before committing to a full implementation.

The SAP Cloud Readiness Assessment is a structured, senior-led evaluation of your current landscape, processes, and data — designed to give you a clear picture of readiness, risk, and the right path forward.

Request the assessment See how it works
The Problem

Most SAP Cloud projects fail before they start.

Companies commit to full implementations without a clear picture of their current landscape. The result: scope surprises, data quality issues, missed timelines, and cost overruns that could have been avoided.

  • Custom ABAP code and modifications that block clean-core adoption
  • Master data quality issues that derail data migration
  • Process gaps that require expensive customization instead of standard SAP
  • Integration dependencies that are undocumented or fragile
  • No clear picture of what a realistic scope and timeline looks like
The Outcome

A clear, honest picture of your readiness — before you commit.

  • Identified risks and blockers — with mitigation strategies
  • Custom code classification and clean-core migration options
  • Data quality assessment and migration approach recommendation
  • Realistic timeline and scope recommendation for your implementation
  • BTP opportunity map for extensions and integrations

What You Receive

Concrete deliverables, not slide decks.

Everything documented and structured so you can act on it — whether you work with us or not.

  • Readiness Report — structured PDF with findings, risk ratings, and recommendations organized by area (data, process, integrations, custom code)
  • Custom Code Classification — inventory of Z-objects classified as retire, keep, or migrate to BTP
  • Scope Boundary Document — realistic scope definition for a SAP Cloud implementation based on your actual landscape
  • Migration Roadmap — phased approach with dependencies, estimated effort ranges, and risk mitigation actions
  • BTP Opportunity Map — recommended extensions, integrations, and automation candidates for your SAP Cloud environment
  • Readout Session — 60-minute call with our senior architect to walk through findings and answer your questions

No implementation commitment required
