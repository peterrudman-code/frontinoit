Our Approach

What makes Frontino IT different.

We are not a staffing agency. We are not a large consultancy. We are a focused SAP delivery partner with a clear methodology and a direct style.

Senior expertise, always

Every engagement is led by architects and consultants with deep SAP, BTP and clean-core experience. The people who scope your project are the people who deliver it.

Clean-core as a standard

We never recommend modifying the ERP core as a first response. Our default is BTP-based extensions that keep the standard upgradeable and the architecture sustainable.

Direct, structured communication

Our clients and partners know what is happening, what was decided, and what comes next — without needing to chase status updates or decode consultant language.

Results before reports

We focus on working solutions, documented processes, and measurable outcomes. The goal is a live system your team can use, not a slide deck explaining why it is complex.

Reusable delivery assets

We bring pre-built integration templates, automation scenarios, UAT scripts, and runbooks to each project — reducing your delivery risk and accelerating go-live timelines.

Focused on SAP

Our work centres on the SAP ecosystem — implementations, BTP, clean core, integration, automation, AI and our own enterprise solutions. That focus means faster ramp-up, fewer escalations and more reliable delivery.

Our Process

How every engagement runs.

Predictable delivery starts with a predictable process. Every project follows the same four-phase structure.

01

Discovery

We assess your current architecture, map core business flows, identify custom code risks, and agree on the right delivery approach before any work starts.

02

Scope & Roadmap

We define a standard-first roadmap with fixed milestones, documented scope boundaries, and clear accountability for every deliverable.

03

Delivery

Our senior architects configure, build, test, and document. No surprises. No scope creep. Regular check-ins with structured status reporting throughout.

04

Handover & Run

We train your team, produce clean runbooks, and transition to monitoring or managed support — so the knowledge stays with you, not us.
