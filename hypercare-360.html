Post Go-Live Support

Stabilize your SAP go-live with functional support, incident control, and user guidance.

Hypercare 360 is a structured post-go-live support model that covers functional triage, incident escalation, user guidance, and process stabilization — so the first weeks after go-live do not become a crisis.

What Goes Wrong

Go-live is not the finish line. It is where the real work starts.

Most SAP projects invest heavily in delivery and training — and then leave the business to fend for itself the moment the system goes live. The result is predictable.

  • Incident tickets pile up faster than the project team can process them
  • Users revert to Excel and email because they cannot navigate the new system under pressure
  • Critical business processes — billing, procurement, logistics — stall because nobody owns the resolution
  • No triage structure — everything is a priority 1, nothing gets resolved fast
  • Leadership loses confidence in the system and in the project team that delivered it
What Hypercare 360 Delivers

A structured safety net for the weeks that matter most.

  • Dedicated functional support team with clear ownership and SLA
  • Structured incident triage with priority classification and escalation paths
  • Daily war room for critical business process monitoring
  • User guidance and floor support to rebuild confidence in the system
  • Weekly status reports and a formal handover report at close
The Structure

Four pillars of the Hypercare 360 model.

Each pillar has a dedicated owner, a defined scope, and a clear escalation path — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Incident Triage

Every ticket is classified by severity (business-critical / functional / guidance) and routed to the right resolver within defined SLA windows.

Daily War Room

A short daily call with functional leads and the hypercare team to review open incidents, escalate blockers, and confirm critical process continuity.

User Guidance

Direct user support for navigation, process questions, and data entry — reducing shadow IT and rebuilding confidence in the new system within the first two weeks.

Executive Reporting

Weekly incident summary and resolution report for leadership — so the business has full visibility on go-live stability without being in the operational weeds.

Stabilization Curve

How a go-live stabilizes with Hypercare 360.

Every SAP go-live follows a predictable pattern. Hypercare 360 compresses the stabilization curve from months to weeks.

Days 1–7

Critical Phase

Highest incident volume. War room active daily. Business-critical process monitoring. All hands on deck.

Days 8–21

Stabilization Phase

Incident volume drops. Pattern tickets identified and resolved systematically. User confidence rebuilding. War room moves to every other day.

Days 22–56

Transition Phase

Residual guidance tickets. Knowledge transfer to internal team. Formal handover report. System operating independently.
