The Problem

Most BTP environments are built well but managed poorly.

The integration and extension work gets done. The ongoing monitoring, documentation, and incident response rarely does. These are the gaps that eventually cause business disruption.

Failures discovered by end users

Integration errors sit undetected in message queues for hours. The first sign of a problem is a call from the warehouse, finance, or a customer — not a system alert.

BTP managed by non-specialists

The internal team maintaining BTP was not involved in the original build and lacks deep integration expertise. Incidents take three times as long to resolve — if they are resolved at all.

Incomplete or missing documentation

iFlows, API endpoints, field mappings, and connection configurations were never formally documented. When the person who built them leaves, the knowledge leaves too.

SAP quarterly updates create uncertainty

Every SAP S/4HANA Cloud quarterly release changes APIs, deprecates functions, and updates authentication. Without pre-release testing, live integrations break after upgrade.

No visibility into platform health

No one has a clear picture of BTP subaccount usage, entitlement consumption, security posture, or service quota thresholds — until something hits a limit and stops working.

Your BTP environment becomes a managed asset, not a maintenance burden.
